Global organizer · Business development

LTC

We identify and organize business opportunities in emerging markets. We work with manufacturers, suppliers, and partners seeking to establish or expand operations in Southeast Asia, Central Asia, South America, and other regions. Rather than advising remotely, we invest our own capital and time to verify opportunities firsthand. We only commit to projects we are confident we can deliver. We operate on a performance basis only. Results determine compensation. This means we are selective about partnerships and require reliability from everyone we work with.

Our Approach

We identify global business opportunities and connect them with proven local partners. Rather than establishing operations in every market, LTC works together with trustworthy regional experts who understand local conditions and regulatory environments. This allows us to move quickly, minimize complexity, and focus on what we do best: sourcing, structuring, and connecting. For day-to-day execution on the ground, LTC works with a dedicated team or partner in each region. This local knowledge and execution capability means efficiency without duplication.

Why We're Different

Many companies see emerging markets as risky or complex. Cultural differences, language barriers, and regulatory unfamiliarity create hesitation. They retreat to easier markets or abandon opportunities altogether.

What others perceive as obstacles, we navigate as opportunity.

We see this differently. We speak the languages. We understand local business culture. We have relationships built over years. This is particularly relevant for companies sourcing from the other side of the world. Whether sourcing, manufacturing, or partnership, entering unfamiliar territories requires more than capital. It requires understanding. We provide that.
